Privacy Policy

This describes what data the Wavelength Discord bot ("Wavelength," "the bot") collects, why, and how it's handled.

§ 01

What Gets Collected

Wavelength only collects what's needed to play and manage music:

DataWhy
Discord user & server IDsRequired by Discord's API for every command; used to know who queued what and which server/channel to play in.
Song/search queries you submitSent to YouTube (via yt-dlp) to resolve and stream the requested audio.
Currently playing track infoTitle, elapsed time, and lyrics position - shown in the bot's now-playing panel and (optionally, if you've enabled it) a linked live-status webpage.

Wavelength does not read or log ordinary server text messages - only the commands you give it.

§ 02

Third-Party Processing

Playback depends on a few external services:

  • YouTube — song queries/URLs are sent to YouTube to locate and stream audio.
  • Spotify (optional sync feature) — if enabled, the bot reads the currently-playing track from a connected Spotify session to mirror it.
  • Lyrics providers — the current track's artist/title is sent to a lyrics lookup service to fetch synced lyrics.
§ 03

Storage & Retention

Wavelength doesn't maintain long-term listening history - queue and playback state exist only while the bot is actively running, and are cleared when it restarts or leaves a voice channel.

§ 04

Live Status Page (Optional)

The bot operator may run a companion webpage showing the current track, progress, and live lyrics in real time, along with the operator's own public Discord username/avatar. This reflects only the currently playing track's public metadata - it does not expose your Discord account, messages, or activity.

§ 05

Children's Privacy

Wavelength is intended for use in accordance with Discord's own Terms of Service, which require users to be at least the minimum age set by Discord. It does not knowingly collect data from users below that age.

§ 06

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Wavelength's features change. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

§ 07

Contact

Data requests or privacy questions can be sent to the developer on Discord: @kochajacy.
